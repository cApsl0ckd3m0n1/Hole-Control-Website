THE ALLIANCE

Good fights.
Stronger community.

Combat is at the heart of what we do. We value capable pilots, coordinated fleets, and the shared experience of living in wormhole space.

Our charter sets a wider purpose: support active life in J-space, develop our members, and help the wormhole community thrive. We bring that commitment to the way we fly and the way we treat others.

LIFE IN J-SPACE

More than the next fight.

The fight gives us a focus. The people beside us give it meaning.

PvP at the centre.

Hole Control brings together pilots who want combat to be a defining part of their time in EVE. Hunting, flying together, and developing as a fleet are at the heart of that shared purpose.

It is a commitment to getting better together: learning from experience, building confidence, and valuing what each pilot brings to the fleet.

A place to develop

Training and shared experience help turn individual capability into coordinated fleets. Member development is part of our wider purpose.

A community worth flying with

Respect matters within the alliance and across the grid. The relationships between pilots are part of what makes life in wormhole space worthwhile.

A stake in wormhole space

Our commitment extends to an active J-space community, with opportunities for pilots to connect, grow, and find their next fight.
